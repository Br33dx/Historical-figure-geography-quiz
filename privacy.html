Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Mapzino (mapzino.com) is a free hobby project with quiz games. This page explains in plain words what data the site handles.

What we collect

Visit statistics. The site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It counts page views and shows things like the page visited, the referring site, the browser and the country. It does not use cookies, does not track you across websites and does not store your IP address in a way that identifies you.

Your settings. Your chosen color scheme is saved in your own browser (local storage). It never leaves your device.

Scores and answers. Games run entirely in your browser. Your guesses and scores are not sent to any server, with one exception: the daily challenge.

Daily challenge standings. If you choose to add your score to the daily standings, the nickname you type, your score and the time are stored in a Google Firebase Realtime Database and shown publicly in that day's Top 10. Please do not use your real name or any personal details as a nickname. We do not collect your email or any other identifier with it. Your browser also remembers your nickname and today's result locally. To have an entry removed, write to feedback@mapzino.com with the nickname and date.

Third-party map data

Posting a daily score and viewing the standings means your browser contacts Google Firebase, which sees your IP address. Some games load a free map library (D3 and Datamaps) from the cdnjs content delivery network run by Cloudflare. Your browser contacts that service to download the files, which means it sees your IP address, as with any website resource. We do not receive any data from it.

Accounts and personal data

There are no accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name or any personal details. A nickname for the daily standings is optional.

Feedback emails

If you write to feedback@mapzino.com, we receive your email address and your message, and use them only to reply to you and to improve the site. We do not share them with anyone.

Advertising

The site currently shows no ads. If ads are added in the future, this page will be updated and, where required, you will be asked for consent first.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, and to complain to your data protection authority. In Slovakia that is the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov). To exercise your rights, email feedback@mapzino.com.
